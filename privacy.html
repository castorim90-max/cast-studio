Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Cast Studio is a personal tool used only by the artist Cast to upload his own videos to his own TikTok account as drafts.

What data we use

When Cast connects his TikTok account, TikTok gives Cast Studio an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar). Cast Studio uses them only to send Cast's videos to his TikTok drafts.

Where it is kept

The token is stored only on Cast's own computer, in a private file. It is not sold, shared or sent to any third party, and it is not used for advertising.

Other people

Cast Studio does not collect data about other TikTok users.

Deleting data

Cast can disconnect Cast Studio at any time from the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions); the local token is then deleted.

Contact

Questions: through the artist's official profiles, for example instagram.com/castreall.
